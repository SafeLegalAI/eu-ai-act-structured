# Agent brief — EU AI Act national implementation register (read AGENT-BRIEF-COMMON.md first, then `schema/member_states.schema.json`)

**Dataset.** For each of the 27 member states plus Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein (EEA), Switzerland and the United Kingdom (parallel approaches): one row per instrument — `implementing-act` (national law implementing/accompanying Reg. 2024/1689: title, status, date), `designation-notice` (market surveillance authority / single point of contact / notifying authority / fundamental-rights authorities under Arts 70 and 77 — the Commission has **not** yet published the MSA list, so use the national notice or the AI Act Service Desk national-resources page https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/national-resources and the Commission's fundamental-rights list https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/fundamental-rights-protection-authorities-ai-act), `penalty-law` (national Art. 99 penalty regime), `sandbox` (Art. 57 regulatory sandbox — **deadline is 2 August 2027 after Reg. (EU) 2026/1744**; record whether one is operational/announced/none), `deployer-guidance` (national guidance relevant to deployers in the administration of justice / legal practice, Annex III pt 8), `omnibus-position` (the state's stated position on the Digital Omnibus), `authority-page` (the authority's own AI Act page when nothing else exists yet).

**Sources.** National gazettes and parliaments (gesetze-im-internet.de / bundesnetzagentur.de for DE — KI-MIG in force 2026-07-29; finlex.fi for FI Law 1377/2025; normattiva.it for IT Legge 132/2025; congreso.es / boe.es for ES; legifrance (403 to bots — Wayback/manual); irishstatutebook.ie (S.I. 366/2025?); retsinformation.dk (Law 467); wetten.overheid.nl; and each authority's own site), the Service Desk national-resources filter, Commission pages. Leads only: Future of Life Institute national implementation plans (artificialintelligenceact.eu), IAPP, Vorp Labs, law-firm trackers. Quote the original-language title (`title_original`) and give an English title (`title_en`, `machine_translation: true` if you translated). `quote_original` ≤25 words in the original language. Gazette texts: DE §5 UrhG, IT L.633/1941 art. 5, ES LPI art. 13, FR Licence Ouverte — official acts are free of copyright; facts always; still quote ≤25 words.

`row_id`: `<state-lower>-<instrument_type>-<slug>` e.g. `de-implementing-act-ki-mig`, `fi-designation-notice-traficom-spoc`, `es-implementing-act-ley-organica-ia-bill`. `summary` ≤50 words. Where a state has nothing yet, write one `authority-page` row with `status: unknown` and what you checked in `notes` — so the state page can say "no national instrument found, checked <date>".
